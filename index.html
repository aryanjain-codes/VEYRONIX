Why re-KYC?

When a bank freezes an account linked to your complaint, it may ask you to re-verify your identity before releasing recovered funds — an RBI-aligned safeguard against the account being taken over a second time.

Three verification methods are available below — face match, fingerprint, or iris/device biometric. Fingerprint and iris use your device's own secure biometric authenticator (the same one behind your phone/laptop unlock) via the browser's WebAuthn standard, so the prompt you see is real, not decorative. VEYRONIX itself never receives your actual fingerprint or iris data — only a pass/fail signal from your device.

Privacy note: this demo performs no real biometric capture, matching or storage. A production build would use UIDAI/RBI-compliant infrastructure, with data minimised per the DPDP Act, 2023.

About & methodology

What this build is, and — just as importantly — what it isn't.

What VEYRONIX is

VEYRONIX (Predictive Risk Engine for Detecting Illicit Cash-out Transactions) is a four-layer predictive-intelligence concept — data fusion, a spatio-temporal risk model, an explainable map, and an alerting/response layer — designed to sit alongside NCRP, CFCFRMS, Pratibimb, Samanvaya and MuleHunter.AI and answer the one question none of them do: where is a fraudulent cash-out about to happen next.

This build, VEYRONIX, is the working student prototype of that idea: HTML/CSS/JS + Leaflet on the front end, a Python/Flask + scikit-learn + SHAP pipeline on the back end in the full architecture, SQLite/CSV for demo storage, and a SHA-256 hash-chain for tamper-evidence — exactly the stack described in Sections 13–14 of the team's research paper.

What it isn't

It is not a live Government of India system, and it is not connected to any real bank, ATM network, or the actual NCRP/CFCFRMS databases. Every location, account number, complaint and victim record shown here is synthetic, generated to reproduce the clustering patterns seen in five independently reported real cases (Srinagar, Khammam, Surat, Bengaluru, Nanded — Section 10) — not real people's data.

A risk score is a lead for a human officer, never proof of a crime, and recovery of stolen funds can never be guaranteed. No biometric data is captured, matched, or stored anywhere in this demo.
